Privacy Policy for SolEctricity (সূর্য বিদ্যুৎ)

Last updated: November 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application SolEctricity (সূর্য বিদ্যুৎ), an engineering and solar sizing utility tool designed for renewable energy modeling and calculations.

1. Information We Collect and Use

Location Data: SolEctricity requests access to your approximate or precise device location solely to fetch localized solar radiation and Peak Sun Hours (PSH) data directly from NASA's solar databases. This location data is processed ephemerally on your device during the calculation request and is never stored, collected, shared, or transmitted to any private third-party server.

Personal Data: The application does not require user registration, logins, or personal identification. We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, or financial information.

2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The application interacts with NASA's open-access solar data services to retrieve solar irradiance metrics. No personally identifiable information (PII) is shared with this service. There are no third-party advertisements or tracking analytics integrated into the application.

3. Data Security and Retention

Since we do not store or transmit personal or location data on external servers, your data remains entirely within your control on your local device.

4. Children’s Privacy

SolEctricity is a technical utility application intended for engineers, technicians, researchers, and adults interested in solar design. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised date.
